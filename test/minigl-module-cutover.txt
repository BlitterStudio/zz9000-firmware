MiniGL stage-4 cutover inventory
=================================

This is deliberately a separate coordinator-owned link change.  It must remove
all resident MiniGL inputs in one commit, after MINIGL.ZZM is packed and
installed in the fixed module slot.  Do not link this list and the module in
one intermediate firmware image.

Remove from ZZ9000OS resident C_SRCS:
  mw_service.c mw_parse.c mw_draw.c mw_texture.c mw_ctx.c
  minigl_transport.c minigl_present.c minigl_raster.c minigl_lighting.c
  minigl_profile.c minigl_rix_backend.c
  rix_cmd.c rix_drv.c rix_setup.c rix_texture.c
  zzapp_gl.c

Remove the resident private-library front-end MGL_FRONT_SRCS/MGL_FRONT_OBJS:
  amiberry_gl_state.c amiberry_gl_draw.c amiberry_gl_texture.c
  amiberry_gl_light.c amiberry_gl_light_get.c amiberry_gl_list.c
  amiberry_gl_buffer.c amiberry_glu.c
  amiberry_backend_inproc.c amiberry_transport_inproc.c

The replacement call paths are the registered zzm_minigl_ops_v1 worker and
lifecycle operations, module transport acquire/release, and app_bind/swap/
unbind/reclaim.  Remove direct scheduler MiniGL polling, direct transport
initialization/descriptor publication, and direct zzapp_gl resident calls at
the same time.  There is no resident fallback.

Stage four acceptance is both standard and RasterIX-EF firmware links within
the 4 MiB high-image gate, plus module size at or below its independent 4 MiB
slot.  The module source/build contract is modules/minigl/module.mk.
